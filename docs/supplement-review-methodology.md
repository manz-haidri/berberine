# Berberine Supplement Editorial Review Methodology

**Document Version:** 2.1  
**Last Audit Date:** 2026-10-25  
**Review Scope:** Editorial Evaluation of Five Commercial Over-the-Counter Berberine Supplements  
**Standard Statement:** Editorial review of five products based on manufacturer labels and publicly available documentation. We do not perform laboratory testing, chemical assays, or clinical outcome trials.

---

## 1. Review Scope and Five-Product Selection

This methodology establishes a structured, reproducible editorial framework for evaluating commercial over-the-counter berberine dietary supplements available in the United States retail market.

### Products Evaluated
1. **Thorne Berberine** (Dual-Action: Berberine HCl + Berberine Phytosome complex, SKU SF800)
2. **Pure Encapsulations Berberine UltraSorb** (Indena Berbevis® phospholipid phytosome complex, SKU 025216)
3. **Dear Apothecary Berberine HCl** (Dual-source root and bark Berberine HCl, SKU 46673-832)
4. **Nature Made Berberine Phytosome** (Berbevis® phospholipid phytosome complex, SKU HL000077)
5. **Nature's Bounty Berberine** (Standard retail Berberine HCl, 1,000 mg, 60ct Retail)

### Formulation and Distribution Categories Represented
- **Chemical Salt Formats:** Conventional crystalline berberine hydrochloride (HCl) salts derived from botanical extracts (*Berberis aristata*).
- **Phospholipid Phytosome Matrices:** Formulations utilizing lipid-complex delivery vehicles (such as Indena Berbevis®) intended to alter gastrointestinal dispersion and cellular membrane permeability.
- **Combination Formulations:** Blends delivering both isolated HCl salts and phospholipid phytosomes within a single dosage unit.
- **Retail Distribution Tiers:** Practitioner direct-dispense channels, specialty direct-to-consumer online brands, and nationwide retail pharmacy/mass-market chains.

## 2. What Was Reviewed and What Was Not Tested

### In Scope (What We Reviewed)
- **Manufacturer Physical Product Labels:** Serving sizes, unit counts, ingredient declarations, declared complex masses, active alkaloid standardization percentages, allergen statements, and suggested directions for use.
- **Official Manufacturer Documentation:** Published technical factsheets (e.g., Pure Encapsulations Product Information Sheets), official product web listings, and corporate quality assurance disclosures.
- **Third-Party Certification Registries:** Verification of claims against public databases including the NSF Certified Dietary Supplements registry (NSF/ANSI Standard 173) and USP Verified listings.
- **Retail Availability & Pricing:** Verified one-time purchase retail prices from direct official manufacturer stores or verified reputable retail partners (with recorded check dates in YYYY-MM-DD format).

### Out of Scope (What Was Not Tested / Explicit Non-Claims)
- **No In-House Analytical Testing:** We have not performed hands-on chemical assays, HPLC chromatography, mass spectrometry (ICP-MS), dissolution testing, or microbial culturing on these products.
- **No Hands-on Clinical Outcome Testing:** We do not conduct human clinical trials or patient health outcome studies on individual commercial bottles.
- **Documentation Scores vs. Clinical Ratings:** Editorial rankings evaluate publicly verifiable documentation, label clarity, testing verification scope, and price transparency. An editorial score does **not** constitute a clinical efficacy rating, a medical safety endorsement, or proof of therapeutic cure.

## 3. Four-Domain Evaluation Rubric (100 Points Total)

Every product is evaluated across four distinct domains, each scored out of 25 points, yielding a total composite score out of 100 points. Totals are calculated dynamically by summing the four domain scores rather than maintaining separate hardcoded numbers.

```
Total Composite Score = Domain 1 (Label Clarity) + Domain 2 (Testing Scope) + Domain 3 (Source Traceability) + Domain 4 (Price Transparency)
```

---

### Domain 1: Label Clarity & Ingredient Disclosure (Max 25 pts)
Evaluates whether packaging explicitly discloses the exact formulation chemistry, botanical source, plant parts, and distinguishes carrier mass from active berberine alkaloid content.

- **21–25 pts (Exceptional Disclosure):** Explicit botanical species (*Berberis aristata*) and plant parts identified; clear formulation type; unambiguous distinction between complex weight and active berberine alkaloid content (or 100% pure declared salt form); clear per-capsule and per-serving breakdown with zero proprietary blends.
- **16–20 pts (High Transparency with Minor Limitation):** Botanical species and carrier components clearly disclosed; active berberine content disclosed via technical factsheet or standardized calculation (though not printed on the front retail panel), or standard isolated salt form clearly stated.
- **11–15 pts (Basic Botanical Identification):** Sourced extract named, but active alkaloid standardization percentage is omitted by the manufacturer from both the retail label and product page (e.g., total phytosome mass declared without specifying active berberine yield).
- **0–10 pts (Opaque or Contradictory Labeling):** Ambiguous proprietary blends, undisclosed botanical parts, or contradictory dosage claims.

---

### Domain 2: Documented Testing & Certification Scope (Max 25 pts)
Evaluates public evidence supporting product quality, purity, and safety. Crucially distinguishes facility-level audit registrations from finished-product certifications, and manufacturer-reported testing from independent third-party seals.

- **21–25 pts (Verified Finished-Product Certification):** Independently verified finished-product certification for the exact SKU listed in a public registry (e.g., active USP Verified Mark or NSF Contents Certified listing for that specific bottle). *None of the five products currently reviewed hold an active finished-product registry certification.*
- **16–20 pts (Verified Independent Facility-Level Registration):** Verified independent registration of the manufacturing facility (e.g., NSF-registered cGMP facility) in an official registry, plus manufacturer-reported comprehensive analytical batch testing (identity, potency, microbial limits, heavy metals).
- **11–15 pts (Manufacturer-Reported Analytical Testing in cGMP Facility):** Manufacturer reports external ISO 17025 accredited laboratory testing (14–15 pts) or internal in-house batch testing under corporate cGMP protocols (11–13 pts), without an independent public facility registration or finished-product registry listing.
- **6–10 pts (General Facility cGMP Assertion):** General manufacturer assertion of facility cGMP compliance without published analytical certificates or third-party verification marks.
- **0–5 pts (Undisclosed / Unverified):** No manufacturing standard or testing information disclosed.

---

### Domain 3: Source Traceability & Consistency (Max 25 pts)
Evaluates the stability and accuracy of manufacturer documentation, consistency across retail listings, and the presence of direct manufacturer specifications.

- **21–25 pts (High Traceability & Direct Sales):** Official manufacturer product page with verified technical factsheet or direct e-commerce checkout; complete consistency between package capsule count, serving counts, and digital specifications; verified single-bottle terms.
- **16–20 pts (Stable Direct Listing):** Direct manufacturer store active with stable product data; single direct distribution channel or standard retail availability; minor historical discrepancies resolved in current revisions.
- **11–15 pts (Informational Site Only / Distributor Dependent):** Brand maintains an informational product page but lacks direct purchase checkout, routing users to external retail partner networks; country of manufacture not declared on bottle.
- **0–10 pts (Conflicting / Broken Specifications):** Conflicting package counts, broken references, or unresolvable discrepancies between marketing copy and physical packaging.

---

### Domain 4: Price & Serving Transparency (Max 25 pts)
Evaluates whether pricing, packaging units, and dosing directions are complete, traceable, and mathematically transparent. 

**Methodological Standard:** Scoring is strictly based on documentation completeness and mathematical transparency—**not** on whether a product costs under $1.00 per serving. Different serving sizes and chemical formulations are not clinically equivalent, and affordability is displayed separately as factual cost information without implying equivalent potency, clinical benefit, or value across differing formulations.

Domain 4 is scored across five objective criteria (up to 5 points each):
1. **Traceable Price Source & Verification Date (0–5 pts):**
   - 5 pts: Direct manufacturer e-commerce store or verified reputable retailer page with recorded verification date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
   - 3 pts: Retail distributor network without direct checkout; price verified via partner survey.
   - 0–1 pts: Unverified or undated pricing.
2. **Identified Package Size & One-Time Purchase Terms (0–5 pts):**
   - 5 pts: Explicit bottle capsule count and clear one-time purchase option with no mandatory subscription or hidden recurring charges.
   - 3–4 pts: One-time purchase available but subscription heavily pushed; terms clear.
   - 0–2 pts: Forced auto-ship enrollment or opaque pricing terms.
3. **Clear Capsule & Labeled Serving Counts (0–5 pts):**
   - 5 pts: Exact capsules per container and explicit servings per container matching mathematical formulation without ambiguity.
   - 3–4 pts: Minor rounding or minor discrepancies between marketing text and label panel.
   - 0–2 pts: Conflicting or missing serving counts.
4. **Mathematically Correct Cost-per-Serving Calculations (0–5 pts):**
   - 5 pts: Exact cost-per-serving derived cleanly from one-time price divided by labeled servings (`priceUSD / servingsPerContainer`).
   - 3–4 pts: Approximate or range-dependent calculation clearly labeled as estimated.
   - 0–2 pts: Contradictory, obscured, or mathematically flawed calculations.
5. **Clear Manufacturer Directions & Daily Cost Transparency (0–5 pts):**
   - 5 pts: Unambiguous manufacturer directions for use printed on packaging, enabling exact daily cost or transparent daily cost range calculation (variable directions earn full points when the resulting daily range is clearly disclosed).
   - 3–4 pts: Directions present but require clinical assumption to calculate daily cost.
   - 0–2 pts: No directions for use provided.

## 4. Missing-Data Treatment and Tie-Breaking Rules

### Missing Data Handling
- **Missing Documentation Is Not Proof of Defect:** The absence of a public certificate of analysis or third-party registry seal does **not** prove that a product is impure or that testing never occurred. Missing evidence is categorized as `"Not publicly verified"` rather than assumed fraudulent, and is evaluated strictly within Domain 2.
- **Unavailable Active Breakdown:** If a manufacturer discloses a complex mass (such as 550 mg phytosome) but does not publish the active alkaloid standardization percentage on its retail label or factsheet, we record: `"Not separately disclosed by manufacturer."` We do not infer standardization from a different brand using a similarly branded ingredient.
- **Unverified Retail Pricing:** Where a manufacturer does not sell direct and prices fluctuate across retail partners, the benchmark price is marked `"Estimated"` and all derived metrics are consistently labeled as estimated.

### Tie-Breaking Hierarchy
When two or more products achieve identical calculated composite rubric scores, rankings are assigned ordinally using the following published tie-breaking rules:
1. **Tie-Breaker 1:** The product with the higher score in **Domain 2 (Documented Testing & Certification Scope)** receives the higher rank.
2. **Tie-Breaker 2:** If still tied, the product with the higher score in **Domain 1 (Label Clarity & Ingredient Disclosure)** receives the higher rank.
3. **Tie-Breaker 3:** If still tied after Domains 2 and 1, a co-ranked editorial tie is declared (e.g., Co-Rank 02).

*Application in Current Review:* Thorne and Pure Encapsulations both achieve a calculated composite score of 82/100. Applying Tie-Breaker 1, Thorne scores 18/25 in Domain 2 (documented NSF facility cGMP registration) while Pure Encapsulations scores 13/25 in Domain 2 (manufacturer-reported testing). Thorne receives Rank 01, and Pure Encapsulations receives Rank 02.

## 5. Limits of Comparing Different Formulations

### Standard Berberine HCl vs. Berberine Phytosome
A foundational rule of this review is: **Never compare phytosome-complex mass against isolated berberine alkaloid content as though they are equivalent.**
- **Berberine HCl:** Delivers isolated crystalline alkaloid salts (e.g., 500 mg, 1,000 mg, or 1,200 mg berberine HCl).
- **Berberine Phytosome:** Binds berberine extract to dietary phospholipids (e.g., sunflower lecithin) along with carrier proteins (e.g., pea protein). A 550 mg phytosome complex typically contains approximately 28% active berberine alkaloid (~154 mg berberine). Comparing a 550 mg complex directly against 1,200 mg of isolated berberine HCl creates a false equivalence.
- **Pharmacokinetics vs. Clinical Endpoints:** While pharmacokinetic trials demonstrate greater circulating plasma AUC for phytosome matrices, direct, double-blind randomized controlled trials comparing disease endpoints (e.g., HbA1c or LDL-C reductions) between matched HCl and phytosome dosages remain limited. Bioavailability enhancements must not be conflated with proven clinical superiority.

## 6. Commercial Independence & Affiliate Policy

- **No Paid Placements or Ranking Purchases:** Product rankings are determined strictly by the four-domain rubric and published tie-breakers. No manufacturer or merchant can purchase a higher rank, favorable review, placement badge, or altered score.

## 7. Maintenance & Re-Verification Standards

- **Audit Trigger:** Product data is audited quarterly, or immediately upon notification of manufacturer packaging redesigns, formula reformulations, or pricing updates.
- **Date Recording:** Every verified claim, price check, and registry search records the exact date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format. Verification dates are updated only when a manual check of the primary source has occurred.
